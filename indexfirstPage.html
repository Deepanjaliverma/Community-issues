How it works

From sighting to solved, in four steps.

No app to install, no office to call. Just report what you see — the community does the rest.

02 — Surface

It goes live

Your report appears instantly on the public dashboard for the whole locality to see.

03 — Upvote

Community backs it

Neighbours facing the same problem upvote it, pushing it up the priority list.

04 — Resolve

Status updates

Track progress as it moves from Pending → In Progress → Resolved.
